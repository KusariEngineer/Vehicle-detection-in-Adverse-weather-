Traffic monitoring has become increasingly important due to the rapid growth of urban populations and vehicle density. Modern cities face severe congestion problems, leading to increased travel time, fuel consumption, and environmental pollution. Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS) aim to provide automated solutions for traffic control, monitoring, and analysis.
Traditional traffic monitoring systems rely on hardware-based sensors such as inductive loops, radar systems, and infrared sensors. Although these methods provide useful data, they suffer from limitations such as high installation cost, limited coverage, and difficulty in mainte- nance. These systems also fail to provide detailed visual information about traffic conditions.
With the advancement of computer vision and deep learning, camera-based traffic monitor- ing systems have become widely popular. These systems analyze video streams in real time to detect vehicles, classify traffic patterns, and estimate vehicle speed. Deep learning models such as Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) have significantly improved the accuracy of object detection tasks.
Among various object detection algorithms, YOLO has gained attention due to its real-time performance and high detection accuracy. However, adverse weather conditions such as fog, rain, and low illumination significantly affect image quality, making it difficult for models to extract meaningful features.
